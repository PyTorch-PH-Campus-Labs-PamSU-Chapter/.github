## 📖 About Our Chapter

The **PyTorch Philippines Campus Labs - Pampanga State University Chapter** (*PyTorch Philippines Campus Labs - PSU Chapter*) is an official student-led technical organization based at Pampanga State University (formerly Don Honorio Ventura State University). 

Operating under the guidance of **PyTorch Philippines Campus Labs**, our chapter serves as an open, cross-disciplinary campus hub where students explore emerging technologies, build practical AI/ML applications, and contribute to open-source software.

---

## 🎯 Mission & Vision

### Mission
To empower students through accessible technical education, collaborative learning, and innovation in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) by providing training opportunities, learning resources, technical projects, and community support centered on the PyTorch framework.

### Vision
To become a leading campus community for AI and ML education and development, where students from diverse academic disciplines can explore emerging technologies, engage in collaborative projects and research, and cultivate technical excellence, creativity, and responsible innovation.

---

## 🧭 Core Values

* **Open-Source Collaboration:** Promoting knowledge-sharing, cooperation, and contributions to open-source technologies and global developer communities.
* **Inclusivity:** Encouraging participation among students of diverse academic backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels in a respectful and supportive environment.
* **Integrity:** Upholding honesty, accountability, transparency, and ethical conduct in all organizational activities and code development.
* **Innovation:** Encouraging creativity, critical thinking, experimentation, and practical solutions to real-world challenges.
* **Service:** Applying technical knowledge and organizational efforts to support the University and contribute positively to the wider community.
