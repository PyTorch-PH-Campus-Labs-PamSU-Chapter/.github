# 📜 Code of Conduct

## PyTorch Philippines Campus Labs — Pampanga State University Chapter

### 1. Our Pledge
We, as members, officers, and contributors of the **PyTorch Philippines Campus Labs - PSU Chapter**, are committed to making participation in our community, technical workshops, open-source repositories, and events a harassment-free and empowering experience for everyone.

We welcome everyone regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation (consistent with **Article I, Section 4** and **Article X, Section 4** of our Chapter Constitution and By-Laws).

---

### 2. Our Standards
We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other students and contributors.
* Respecting differing viewpoints, technical opinions, and learning curves.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
* Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall chapter and student learning community.

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment, intimidation, or coercion.
* Publishing others' private information (such as physical or email addresses) without explicit permission.
* Any form of **hazing, initiation rites, humiliation, or abusive behavior** (strictly prohibited under **Article X, Section 3** of the Chapter Constitution and applicable University regulations).
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional, academic, or collaborative setting.

---

### 3. Enforcement Responsibilities & Scope
The **Executive Board** and the **Technical Core** leadership are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This Code of Conduct applies within all chapter-managed spaces:
* Official GitHub repositories, issue forms, pull requests, and discussions.
* Physical campus workshops, hackathons, and laboratory sessions.
* Virtual communication channels (Discord, Messenger, Google Meet, etc.).
* Any scenario where an individual officially represents the chapter in public or university events.

---

### 4. Reporting & Grievances
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by submitting a written notice to the **Unit Head on Records** or the **Director / Executive Board** in accordance with **Article X, Section 6** of the Chapter Constitution.

* **Chapter Email:** `pytorchcampuslabs.psuchapter@gmail.com`
* **Direct Escalation:** If an issue involves an Executive Board officer or presents a conflict of interest, it may be escalated to the chapter's **Faculty Advisers** or the **University Office of Student Affairs**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Retaliation against any individual who reports a violation in good faith is strictly prohibited.
